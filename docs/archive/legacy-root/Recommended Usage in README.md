### Videos

**1. Double Spot Phenomenon** (`videos/double_spot.mp4`)  
When an additional polarizing film is shaken in front of the output assembly, a secondary circular spot appears and disappears repeatedly (blinking effect). This is likely due to polarization modulation and multiple reflections.

**2. Internal Interference Dynamics** (`videos/circular_interference.mp4`)  
At output position near 0°, small angular movements of the nail clipper cause continuous evolution of complex interference patterns inside the main laser spot, demonstrating high sensitivity to alignment in the near-field regime.
