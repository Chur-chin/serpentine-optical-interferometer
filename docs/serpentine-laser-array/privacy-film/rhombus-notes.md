# Laser Mode-Locking Experiment with Privacy Film — Rhombus (마름모)

## Observation Summary

### 1. 0° Orientation
Privacy protection film (사생활보호필름) at 0° exhibits **extremely static and superior mode-locking** compared to standard polarizing film, with mostly single-dot patterns and minimal fluctuation.

### 2. Rhombus (마름모) Configuration
Contrary to expectations, the privacy film shows **almost no change** even in rhombus orientation. Because light does not spread diffusely like in polarizing film, **discrete patterns appear more clearly and stably**.

**Key Advantage**: Privacy film maintains sharp, discrete mode-locked dots across different geometries (0° and rhombus), making it significantly more effective for stable pattern control.
