# Laser Mode-Locking Experiment with Privacy Film

## Observation Summary
Contrary to initial expectations, the **privacy protection film (사생활보호필름)** at 0° orientation demonstrates **superior static behavior** and stronger mode-locking compared to standard polarizing film.

### Key Findings from Videos (v1~v5):
- v1~v2: Extremely static state with only 1~2 dots visible.
- v3: Brief appearance of a small cluster (transient instability).
- v4~v5: Rapid return to and maintenance of a single fixed dot.

**Conclusion**: Privacy film at 0° achieves a much more "mode-locked" and stable regime than general polarizing film, as clearly visible in the experimental footage.
