# Laser Mode-Locking Experiment with Privacy Film — 180°

## Observation Summary

### 1. 0° Orientation
Privacy protection film shows extremely static and superior mode-locking with mostly single-dot patterns.

### 2. Rhombus Configuration
Privacy film maintains sharp discrete patterns with almost no light diffusion.

### 3. 180° Orientation
Privacy protection film at 180° also exhibits **highly stable discrete patterns**.

**Overall Conclusion**:
- For open Serpentine structures, standard polarizing film performs better.
- However, in honeycomb structures, **privacy protection film** provides superior stability across various angles (0°, 180°, Rhombus), maintaining clear discrete mode-locked patterns.
